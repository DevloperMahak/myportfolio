AVIXA HRMS - Account Deletion

This page explains how AVIXA HRMS users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Step 1: Send an email to the AVIXA HRMS support team using the email address registered with your AVIXA HRMS account.
Step 2: Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - AVIXA HRMS
Step 3: Include your Company Code, Login ID, registered email address, and your request to delete your AVIXA HRMS account.
Step 4: Our team may verify the request to ensure that the request is made by the account owner or an authorized user.
Submit your deletion request to:
avixa.hrms@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When an account deletion request is approved, AVIXA HRMS will delete or anonymize personal information associated with the user's account where applicable.

  • Account and login information
  • Personal profile information
  • Profile photo, where applicable
  • Contact information associated with the account
  • Other personal information that is no longer required

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained when required for legitimate business purposes, security, legal obligations, financial or payroll records, dispute resolution, or other applicable requirements.

Where retention is required, the applicable information will be retained only for the necessary period and handled according to applicable privacy and legal requirements.

Deletion Requests

Account deletion requests are reviewed before processing. Additional information may be requested to verify ownership of the account and prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

Important: Deleting an account may remove access to AVIXA HRMS services associated with that account. Some company or legally required records may remain where retention is necessary.

Contact

For account deletion requests or questions regarding your personal data, contact:

AVIXA HRMS Support
Email: avixa.hrms@gmail.com
